Why Food Redistribution Matters

Nearly one-third of all food produced globally is wasted, while millions struggle to secure daily nutritious meals. Organic waste decomposing in landfills produces potent methane gas, driving climate change.

Dignified Food Access

Carbon Footprint Reduction
