Simple & Transparent Process

How Food Redistribution Works

A closed-loop platform ensuring safe, timely, and organized food transfer from surplus kitchens to individuals and registered charitable causes.

01

1. Donors Publish Surplus

Restaurants, hostels, caterers, and bakeries list available surplus food with quantity, dietary classification (veg/non-veg), packaging, pickup window, and mandatory food safety confirmations.

Outcome: Immediate listing publication to verified nearby recipients.
02

2. Browse & Request

Charitable NGOs and individual recipients browse available donations, specify the required quantity, and submit an intended collection time within the donor's designated pickup hours.

Outcome: Donor is notified instantly to review and reserve the food.
03

3. Pickup & Handover

Upon donor approval, the remaining quantity is safely reserved. The recipient arrives at the facility with appropriate clean containers, collects the food, and both parties confirm completion on the platform.

Outcome: Zero food waste, complete audit trail, and instant impact reporting.

Designed For Three Key Roles

Food Donors

Restaurants, hotels, convention centers, and caterers can turn unavoidable surplus into social good without operational headaches.

NGOs & Charities

Verified social organizations can collect large batch donations to feed children's homes, elder care facilities, and relief camps.

Individual Recipients

Individuals and families facing food insecurity can browse dignified, wholesome meals with strict privacy protection.
